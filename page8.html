Ohio's EdChoice Scholarship Program helps eligible families access private education by providing scholarship funding toward tuition.

At Christian Star Academy, all currently enrolled students participate in EdChoice. We encourage all prospective families to explore the program early, as scholarship assistance can significantly reduce their tuition responsibility.

Families may begin preparing their state account and necessary documentation while applying to CSA. Once a student has been accepted, our staff can help guide the family through the school-based scholarship application process.

We understand that every family's circumstances are different. Any remaining tuition balance after scholarships may be paid through one of the following arrangements:
- Annually
- Semi-Annually
- Quarterly
- Monthly
Our staff is happy to help you understand the payment arrangements available to your family.

Preparing for Your EdChoice Scholarship

At Christian Star Academy, all currently enrolled students participate in Ohio's EdChoice Scholarship Program.
EdChoice is an important part of making Christ-centered education accessible to our families, and we encourage every prospective family to begin learning about the program early.
You do not need to wait until your CSA application is complete to begin preparing for EdChoice.
Families can review scholarship information, create their Ohio account, and prepare the required information while exploring enrollment at CSA.
